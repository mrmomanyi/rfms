Methodology

How tree records become carbon and oxygen figures, and where the limits are.

1. What counts as carbon removal

Trees registered as afforestation or reafforestation are planted trees, and the carbon they store counts as removal. Trees registered as existing forest are shown as carbon stored, but not as removal, because planting did not create them. Dead and rejected trees are not counted.

2. Tree size

When a tree has a field measurement, the latest trunk diameter (and height, if given) is used. This is labelled measured. If only diameter is known, height is estimated from it.

Without a measurement, size is estimated from the tree's age with a growth curve. This is labelled modelled and has low confidence. Age comes from the planting date, or the registration date when no planting date is given. An existing tree with no known age and no measurement is not estimated at all, and is listed as needing a measurement.

Generic curve in use unless a species has its own: maximum diameter {{ defaults.dbh_max_cm }} cm, maximum height {{ defaults.height_max_m }} m. These generic values are illustrative and are being replaced by species values.

3. Biomass and carbon

Above-ground biomass uses the pantropical equation of Chave and colleagues (2014):

biomass (kg) = 0.0673 × (wood density × diameter² × height) ^ 0.976

Diameter is in cm, height in metres and wood density in g/cm³. Roots are added with a below-ground to above-ground ratio of {{ root_shoot }}. Carbon is {{ carbon_fraction }} of dry biomass, and CO₂ equivalent is carbon × {{ co2_per_carbon|floatformat:3 }} (the ratio of the molecular weights of CO₂ and C).

The default wood density ({{ default_density }} g/cm³) and the root ratio are placeholders until species and forest-type values are confirmed.

4. Oxygen

Photosynthesis releases about {{ o2_per_co2|floatformat:2 }} kg of oxygen for every kg of CO₂ fixed. The oxygen figure is that amount for the carbon in the tree. Over a whole ecosystem, decay and respiration use most of that oxygen again, so it should be read as oxygen released while the biomass was built, not as oxygen supplied to people.

5. Confidence score

A transparent rule, not a statistical error estimate: modelled only 35; measured diameter 70; measured diameter and height 85; minus 15 if the measurement is older than 24 months; plus 10 if a reviewer has verified the tree (maximum 95).

6. Forecasts

The five-year forecast follows the growth curve, scaled so that year zero matches the tree's current estimate. It assumes the tree survives and is a projection, not a measurement.

7. Rankings

Contributors are ranked by the CO₂e stored in the trees they planted, then by tree count. {% if rank_verified_only %}Only verified trees are counted in rankings.{% else %}All living trees that are not rejected are counted, and the share that is verified is shown.{% endif %} Names are matched without regard to capital letters, so different spellings count as different people.
