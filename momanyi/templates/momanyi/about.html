About RFMS

A robust forest monitoring system for recording trees, measuring their growth, and turning those records into carbon and oxygen estimates.

What it does

Contributors register each tree with its species, exact location and planting details. Measurements are added over time. Reviewers verify records. The dashboards then show what the trees store and release.

What we promise

Every figure comes from a method you can read. Estimates are labelled as estimates. Measured and verified trees are shown separately from modelled ones.
